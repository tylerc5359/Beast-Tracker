## 🧠💡 WHAT IS THIS THING???

Beast Media Tracker is a browser-based personal media library. You can chuck your media into a colourful tile shelf, organise it into categories, give it star ratings, track your progress, and rearrange everything until your eyeballs are satisfied. 👀✅

Basically: **your media collection, but make it a shelf-shaped chaos machine.** 🎉

## 🚀 HOW TO START THE BEAST

No complicated wizard rituals. No twelve-step moon dance. 🌝

1. Download or clone this project.
2. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `app.js` together in the same folder. THEY ARE FRIENDS. 🤝
3. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
4. Press **Edit** and start adding your media. WOOOO!!! 🥳

There is no build step or package installation required for the basic app.

## 🧃 FEATURES, FEATURES, FEATURES!!!

### 🗂️ Your very own media shelf
- Add movies, TV shows, anime, manga, books, albums, games, or whatever media-shaped thing your heart desires.
- Give entries titles, descriptions, categories and sub-categories.
- Add cover art by uploading an image or using the cover-art search option.
- Make simple image-only tiles when you just want the picture. Minimal effort. Maximum picture. 🖼️

### ⭐ RATING TIME, BABYYYY
- Rate entries from one to five stars.
- Track progress from 0% to 100% with a progress bar.
- Add a little progress note, like `Season 2, ch. 14`.
- Give tiles their own colours. COLOUR!!! 🌈

### 🧱 TILE TETRIS (BUT IT'S YOUR LIBRARY)
- Resize tiles into different shapes.
- Drag tiles around in Edit mode to make your own layout.
- Add new tiles in specific empty spots.
- Drop multiple image files onto empty spots to make image tiles. PLONK PLONK PLONK. 🫳
- The layout adapts when the browser window changes size.

### 🔎 FIND THE THING
- Search your library by title or description.
- Filter by category and sub-category.
- Sort by newest, oldest, recently edited, title, rating or progress.
- Use Custom sorting for your own shelf arrangement.

### 🖌️ MAKE IT LOOK LIKE YOUR BRAIN
- Switch between light and dark themes.
- Add multiple background images or GIFs to different parts of the shelf.
- Adjust background blur, brightness and saturation.
- Watch the backgrounds blend as you scroll. OOOOOOOH. ✨

### 📦 BACKUP THE TREASURE
- Export your library as `beast-media-backup.json`.
- Import a previous backup to restore your entries and organisation.
- Your entries, categories, custom order, layout and background settings are included in the backup.

### 🧪 SCIENCE MODE
- Press **Add test tiles** to add 60 test entries.
- Press the button again to remove those test entries.
- Perfect for testing layouts. Not actual wildlife. 🧫🐻

## 🕹️ THE BUTTONS, EXPLAINED FOR TINY GOBLINS

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | Switches between light and dark mode. 🌞🌚 |
| **Select** | Lets you select multiple tiles for bulk actions. 👆 |
| **Categories** | Opens the category manager. 🗃️ |
| **Export** | Downloads a JSON backup. 📤 |
| **Import** | Loads a JSON backup. 📥 |
| **Add test tiles** | Adds or removes 60 test tiles. 🧪 |
| **Edit** | Turns on shelf editing, tile rearranging and adding. ✏️ |

Click a tile to edit its details. Click the stars to rate it. Drag the progress bar to update progress. It's basically a tiny media control centre. 🧑‍🚀

## 💾 WHERE DOES MY STUFF GO???

The app saves its library and settings in your browser's **local storage** and **IndexedDB**. That means your collection is stored locally in that browser, rather than automatically syncing to an online account.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT LITTLE BRAIN NOTE:** browser data can be cleared, and local data does not magically follow you to another device. Use **Export** every now and then to keep a backup. BE NICE TO YOUR FUTURE SELF. 🥺💾
